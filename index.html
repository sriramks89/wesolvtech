Our Approach

AI-native. Human-vetted.

We didn't bolt AI onto an old process. WeSolv is built AI-native from the ground up, with people making the judgment calls that matter.

What AI Does

Sources candidates from relevant talent pools. Scores every candidate against your job description. Flags resume gaps and inconsistencies. Runs and grades coding assessments and scenario tests consistently, every time. Surfaces shortcomings a manual screen would miss.

What Humans Do

Vet every AI-flagged gap before it reaches you. Conduct or oversee final assessments. Validate scores and sign off on every profile. Make the judgment calls AI shouldn't make alone.

The result: nothing falls through the cracks, and nothing gets decided without a person behind it.

How It Works

Meet only fully-vetted candidates, backed by documentation.
